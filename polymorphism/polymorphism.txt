Polymorphism is one of the 4 pillars of object-oriented programming languages where poly means many and morphism means transforming one form into another. 
Polymorphism means the same function with different signatures is called many times. It allows methods to do different things based on the object it is acting upon.

In JavaScript, polymorphism works in two primary ways:

Method Overriding: A child class overrides a method of its parent class.
Method Overloading (simulated): A function behaves differently based on the number or type of its arguments.
